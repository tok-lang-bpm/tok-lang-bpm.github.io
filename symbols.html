tok: Process Models as Text

A BPMN symbols

Every BPMN 2.0.2 symbol that tok draws, with the statement that produces it and whether Camunda 8 executes it. The pictures are made by the tok engine itself: each one comes from a small model exported with tok bpmn and tok svg and cut to the element (tools/symbols.py of the tok repository), so a symbol here looks exactly as it does in your diagrams.

In the column tok, words in capitals (ID, ACTIVITY, P) stand for identifiers of your model, and the values ("M", P1D) are examples. Not executed means that Camunda 8 does not run the element; in an executable document tok check reports the code given (Chapter 10). Modelling only elements are exported and kept, and the engine ignores them. Groups (group ID "Label" members="A B") are exported as BPMN groups without a shape in the diagram, so they have no symbol here.
